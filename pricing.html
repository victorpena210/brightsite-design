How the Subscription Works

The initial commitment covers the custom build while giving you a predictable monthly payment instead of a large upfront invoice.

1. Initial 12-Month Term

The website subscription is $150 per month for an initial 12-month commitment, for a total initial cost of $1,800.

2. After 12 Months

After the initial 12 months are completed, the subscription continues month-to-month at $150 per month until canceled.

3. If You Cancel Early

You may end the subscription before the initial term is complete by paying the remaining balance of the 12-month commitment.

4. If You Leave BrightSite

Once the initial commitment has been paid in full, you may request the final static website files and move the site to another host. Migration or setup with another provider may be quoted separately.
