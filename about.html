About BrightSite Design

Hi, I’m Victor Peña.

BrightSite Design is a San Antonio web design business that I own and operate. I create custom, mobile-friendly websites for small businesses and provide the hosting, updates, security, and ongoing support needed to keep them running.

Before starting BrightSite Design, I helped maintain WordPress and Joomla websites at an advertising agency. That experience taught me that a good business website needs more than attractive colors—it needs clear information, simple navigation, dependable technology, and an obvious next step for the customer.

You work directly with me throughout the project. There are no account managers or handoffs between departments. I handle the planning, development, launch, and continued support myself.
